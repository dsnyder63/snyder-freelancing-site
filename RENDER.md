# Snyder Freelancing — static hosting

Private repo for Snyder Freelancing client pages.

## Deploy
Each folder is its own Render static site, publish path set to that folder, auto-deploy on push to main.
- `cake-factory-sign/` → service `snyder-freelancing-site` → The Cake Factory quote & sign page
- `massage-sign/` → service `massage-sign` → Massage referral quote & sign page
- `stay-gold-sign/` → service `stay-gold-sign` → Stay Gold Apparel quote and mockup page for Jeff
- `stay-gold/` → service `stay-gold` (srv-dbvpctafngtc73a1tmbg) → Stay Gold Apparel storefront, live 2026-11-19 at https://stay-gold.onrender.com
- `snyder-freelancing-pricing/` → service `snyder-freelancing-pricing` (srv-dc031aqjnfac738r32ng) → Stay Gold Apparel Pricing Tool, a Snyder Freelancing tool for Jeff, live 2026-11-20 at https://snyder-freelancing-pricing.onrender.com
- `katie-rose-pricing/` → service `katie-rose-pricing` (srv-dc0ejjp42hec73b5gujg) → Katie Rose Kreations Pricing Tool for Emma, same calculator with bakery cost lines and KRK branding, live 2026-11-20 at https://katie-rose-pricing.onrender.com
- `katie-rose-timers/` → service `katie-rose-timers` → Katie Rose Kreations Oven Timers & Conversions for Emma (up to 6 oven timers plus a conversion calculator: volume, weight, oven temperature, cups-to-grams staples), live 2026-11-22 at https://katie-rose-timers.onrender.com
